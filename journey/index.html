    Six signs of dirty fuel

    Be honest. Nobody is watching.

    Forget the number. One of these hitting hard can matter more than four hitting slightly. Ask yourself: am I running on dirty fuel? Notice the first answer that comes up.

    How this works

    This is one landscape. Water flows downhill.

    1. The mountain is your past experiences, as they live in you now. The highest points are childhood.

    2. The streams are your beliefs: the conclusions a younger you drew about what you are worth, what is safe, and what you must do to deserve love, respect, approval or security.

    3. The river is your state: how it actually feels, day to day, to be you.

    4. The sea is your behavior: what the fuel makes you do, and what you can never quite make yourself do.

    Most people stand by the sea with a bucket. Deeper change happens upstream. Start where you feel it (the river), then trace it up to the belief, then to the moment on the mountain where that belief first made sense.
