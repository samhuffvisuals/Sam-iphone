How this works

This is one landscape. Water flows downhill.

1. The mountain is your past experiences, as they live in you now. The highest points are childhood.

2. The streams are your beliefs: the conclusions a younger you drew about what you are worth, what is safe, and what you must do to deserve love, respect, approval or security.

3. The river is your state: how it actually feels, day to day, to be you.

4. The sea is your behavior: what the fuel makes you do, and what you can never quite make yourself do.

Most people stand by the sea with a bucket. Deeper change happens upstream. Start where you feel it (the river), then trace it up to the belief, then to the moment on the mountain where that belief first made sense.

Map: drag with one finger to turn the landscape, pinch to zoom, drag with two fingers to slide it. Hold a spot to write there. Tap an entry to edit it. Tap 1, 2, 3, 4 to fly to each level.

Journal: everything you write on the map, as one organized list by level. Tap an entry to edit it, or Find on map to fly to it.

Brainstorm: a scratch space for rough ideas about all four parts: mountain, streams, river, sea. Nothing there is on the map. Move an idea to the map when it feels true, or move a map entry back to Brainstorm when you are not sure about it yet.

Chat: a guide who knows the whole training and, if you allow it, what you have written on the map. Talk through what is going on and it will help you trace it upstream, sit with it, and find the belief underneath. It runs on Claude with your own API key, so it needs a connection.

Offline: open the app once with a connection and it is saved to your phone. After that everything except Chat works with no WiFi at all. Your entries always stay on the phone.
