Make it an Apple Music playlist

Apple doesn't let a web page drop a playlist into your library, so there are two ways to get these songs in. The first takes about two minutes.

1. Import the whole list at once

  1. Tap Copy list below. It copies every song as Artist - Title, one per line.
  2. Open TuneMyMusic (or Soundiiz) in Safari, choose Text / Upload from text as the source, and paste.
  3. Pick Apple Music as the destination, sign in, and name the playlist Second Star.
  4. It matches each line to a track. Skim the matches once: for the older records it sometimes picks a re-recording, so swap in the original where it matters.

2. Add them one at a time

  1. In Apple Music, go to Library → Playlists → New Playlist and call it Second Star.
  2. Back here, tap ▶ on a song. Apple Music opens on a search for it.
  3. Touch and hold the track → Add to a Playlist → Second Star.
  4. Come back and tap the circle on the right to mark it added. Your progress is saved on this phone.

Good to know

The sound you are after is called close harmony: three or four voices stacked a few notes apart and sung soft, the way barbershop quartets and the 1930s sister acts did it, which Disney\'s studio choirs (the Ken Darby Singers, Jud Conlon\'s Rhythmaires, the Mellomen) turned into the Peter Pan sound. The list runs 1939 to 1969 because the sound is older than the sixties; the sixties groups that kept it are in section V.
