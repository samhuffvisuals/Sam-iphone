# Second Star

A dreamy fall playlist for Apple Music in **old close harmony**: 100 records that sound like "The Second Star to the Right" from Peter Pan and "When You Wish Upon a Star" from Pinocchio. Open `/playlist/` on the Pages site.

## What that sound is called

**Close harmony.** Three or four voices stacked a few notes apart and sung soft and blended, so they sound like one instrument. Barbershop quartets and the 1930s sister acts (the Boswell Sisters) started it; big-band vocal groups like the Pied Pipers, the Modernaires and the Ink Spots put it on records; and the Hollywood studio choirs turned it into the Disney sound. "When You Wish Upon a Star" is Cliff Edwards with the Ken Darby Singers. "The Second Star to the Right" is the Jud Conlon Chorus and the Mellomen. In the fifties the Four Freshmen, Four Lads, Four Aces, Chordettes, McGuire Sisters and Platters kept it going. In the sixties it lived on in the Lettermen, the Beach Boys (who learned it from the Four Freshmen), the Vogues, Nashville's Jordanaires and Anita Kerr Singers, and sunshine pop like the Association, the Mamas & the Papas and the Free Design.

The full song list is in [playlist.txt](playlist.txt), one `Artist - Title` per line, in six movements:

1. **Wish upon a star**: the Disney and Hollywood originals, sung by the studio choirs.
2. **The Pied Pipers years**: 1940s big-band vocal groups and crooners with a choir behind them.
3. **Golden-age harmony**: the 1950s quartets and sister acts.
4. **Leaves**: the fall songs in that style. Turning leaves, green fields gone brown, a blustery day in the Hundred Acre Wood.
5. **Sixties harmony**: the groups that carried the sound into the sixties.
6. **Goodnight**: lullabies in close harmony to close the record.

Years run 1939 to 1969, because the sound is older than the sixties.

## Put it on your iPhone Home Screen

1. GitHub Pages must serve the branch this app is on: **Settings → Pages → Deploy from a branch**, pick the branch and `/ (root)`, then **Save**.
2. In **Safari** on your iPhone, open the Pages URL with `/playlist/` on the end, for example `https://samhuffvisuals.github.io/Sam-iphone/playlist/`.
3. Tap **Share → Add to Home Screen**.

## Getting it into Apple Music

Apple does not let a web page create a playlist in your library, so the page gives you two routes.

**Import the whole list at once (about two minutes).** Tap **Copy list**, open [TuneMyMusic](https://www.tunemymusic.com/transfer) or Soundiiz in Safari, choose **Text** as the source, paste, pick **Apple Music** as the destination, and name the playlist *Second Star*. Skim the matches once: for the soundtrack songs and the oldest records it sometimes picks a later re-recording, so swap in the original where it matters.

**Add them one at a time.** Make a new playlist called *Second Star* in Apple Music. Back on the page, tap **▶** on a song and Apple Music opens on a search for it. Touch and hold the track → **Add to a Playlist**. Tap the circle on the right of the song to mark it added. Progress is saved on your phone.
