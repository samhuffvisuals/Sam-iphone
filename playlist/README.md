# Second Star

A 1960s dreamy fall playlist for Apple Music: 76 old records that sound like **"The Second Star to the Right"** from Peter Pan. Strings, hushed harmonies, celesta, and leaves coming down. Open `/playlist/` on the Pages site.

The full song list is in [playlist.txt](playlist.txt), one `Artist - Title` per line, grouped into seven movements:

1. **Second star**: the Peter Pan records the list is tuned to, plus the doo-wop and ballads with stars in the lyrics.
2. **Golden hour**: lush early-sixties ballads for the late afternoon.
3. **Leaves**: the fall songs proper. September, turning leaves, geese going south.
4. **Rain on the roof**: for the grey days.
5. **Across the water**: a few from Paris and Milan that drift the same way. Skip it if you want English only.
6. **Starlight**: late-sixties dreamers and the jazz that sounds like snow globes.
7. **Goodnight**: lullabies to close the record.

Tracks 1 to 3 are from 1953 and 1954: the Disney soundtrack choir and Doris Day singing "The Second Star to the Right", and Mary Martin's "Never Never Land". Everything else is 1958–1969.

## Put it on your iPhone Home Screen

1. GitHub Pages must serve the branch this app is on: **Settings → Pages → Deploy from a branch**, pick the branch and `/ (root)`, then **Save**.
2. In **Safari** on your iPhone, open the Pages URL with `/playlist/` on the end, for example `https://samhuffvisuals.github.io/Sam-iphone/playlist/`.
3. Tap **Share → Add to Home Screen**.

## Getting it into Apple Music

Apple does not let a web page create a playlist in your library, so the page gives you two routes.

**Import the whole list at once (about two minutes).** Tap **Copy list**, open [TuneMyMusic](https://www.tunemymusic.com/transfer) or Soundiiz in Safari, choose **Text** as the source, paste, pick **Apple Music** as the destination, and name the playlist *Second Star*. Skim the matches once: for the older records it sometimes picks a re-recording.

**Add them one at a time.** Make a new playlist called *Second Star* in Apple Music. Back on the page, tap **▶** on a song and Apple Music opens on a search for it. Touch and hold the track → **Add to a Playlist**. Tap the circle on the right of the song to mark it added. Progress is saved on your phone.
