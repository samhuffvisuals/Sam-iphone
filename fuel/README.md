# Fuel

A very simple iPhone web app for uncovering your **dirty fuel**. One mountain, a handful of streams running down into a lake. Each stream is one thing that drives you. Zoom into a stream, write down what was really going on, zoom back out and look at the whole mountain.

Clean fuel is love, curiosity, purpose, service. Dirty fuel is fear, shame, approval, proving a point. Dirty fuel streams run brown and murky. Clean ones run clear and blue.

## Put it on your iPhone Home Screen

1. GitHub Pages must serve the branch this app is on: **Settings → Pages → Deploy from a branch**, pick the branch and `/ (root)`, then **Save**.
2. In **Safari** on your iPhone, open the Pages URL with `/fuel/` on the end, for example `https://samhuffvisuals.github.io/Sam-iphone/fuel/`.
3. Tap **Share → Add to Home Screen**.

Open it from the Home Screen icon. The Home Screen app keeps its own storage, separate from Safari.

## Using it

- **Tap a stream** (or its signpost) to zoom in on it. Pinch and drag to look around. Double-tap empty ground to zoom in there.
- **+ Add a note** writes a journal entry onto that stream. A question appears above the box to get you started. Tap ↻ for a different one.
- Notes are pinned along the stream like tags. Tap a tag to edit or delete it.
- **Clean / Dirty** switches what kind of fuel the stream carries. Tap the stream's name to rename or delete it.
- **Zoom out** (top right) returns to the whole mountain.
- **+ Stream** adds a new stream, up to eight. The app starts with six: Curiosity, Proving them wrong, Love of the craft, Fear of falling behind, Helping people, Need for approval. Rename them to match your life.
- **All notes** lists every entry, newest first. Tap one to jump to its stream.
- **⋯ → Back up** saves everything to a file. **Restore** loads it back. Notes live only on your phone, so back up now and then.
