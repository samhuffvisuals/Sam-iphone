# Leads

An Instagram-style iPhone web app with one job: keep track of video-project leads and tag each one 🔥 Hot, ☀️ Warm or ❄️ Cold.

## Put it on your iPhone Home Screen

1. Turn on GitHub Pages for this repo: **Settings → Pages → Build and deployment → Deploy from a branch**, pick this branch and `/ (root)`, then **Save**.
2. Open the Pages URL it shows you (something like `https://samhuffvisuals.github.io/Sam-iphone/`) in **Safari** on your iPhone.
3. Tap **Share → Add to Home Screen**.

Open it from the Home Screen icon before adding leads. The Home Screen app keeps its own storage, separate from Safari.

## Using it

- **+** (top right, tab bar, or the "New lead" circle) adds a lead: name, project type, budget, shoot date, phone, email, Instagram and notes.
- The story circles at the top filter the feed: All, Hot, Warm, Cold.
- Each lead card has Hot / Warm / Cold buttons to retag it. Double-tap the colored card to cycle through them.
- Call, text and email buttons show up on the card when the lead has a phone number or email.
- The 🔥 tab shows only Hot leads. Search finds leads by name, project, notes or contact info.
- **Profile → Back up** saves your leads to a file (Files, AirDrop, email). **Restore** loads a backup.

Leads are stored only on your phone. Back them up now and then.
